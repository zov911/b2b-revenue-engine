# Changelog

## [2.0.0] - 2026-11-11

### Added
- **Show how it works**: a 20-second, 5-step animation of leads leaking between teams, the price of each leak, the yearly total and the fix. Uses the current audit numbers; closes with Esc.
- **Leak audit**: 4 inputs and 7 leaks produce a yearly cost of inaction, a ranked breakdown and a Smarketing alignment score (0–100).
- Every dollar leak has an editable assumption and a named source (Coe 2003, Cognism 2021).
- **Show leaks** mode on the map: leaking hand-offs turn red and drip dollars.
- **Industry presets**: B2B SaaS, Manufacturing (field case: architectural metal manufacturer), Professional services.
- Detail panel tabs: **Flow**, **Sales science** (Atkinson, Cognism, Coe, Hall) and **What zov911 automates**.
- Exports: action plan as Markdown, PDF report, share link that restores your numbers, LinkedIn score post.
- zov911 card with a free 20-min audit and a Revenue Sprint outline; links to related zov911 tools.
- "Price this leak" on every friction card; mobile summary bar; keyboard access to nodes.

### Changed
- Removed unsourced percentages from node and customer copy.
- Lead-response SLA set to 24 hours to match the source.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-03

- Interactive map of 6 marketing, 6 sales and 5 customer nodes with friction points.
