# B2B Revenue Engine: Sales × Marketing × Customer Process Connector

**Live demo:** https://zov911.github.io/b2b-revenue-engine/

An interactive map of how B2B marketing, sales and customer activities feed each other. Click any node (content marketing, paid ads, cold outreach, demos, account expansion, case studies…) to see:

- **What it boosts** downstream
- **What it needs** from the other teams to work well
- **Which metrics** prove it's working
- The **customer intelligence loop** (testimonials, case studies, referrals, product feedback)
- **Friction points:** where revenue leaks between teams

## Why it exists

Most revenue problems sit in the hand-offs between marketing, sales and customer success, not inside any one team. This tool makes those dependencies visible, so it works as a workshop aid, an onboarding resource and a RevOps alignment conversation starter.

## Tech

A single `index.html`: vanilla JS with an interactive SVG connection map and no dependencies.
